For grown-ups and teachers

Wonder Woods supports Understanding the World in Cambridge Early Years, for Play Group learners (Stage 1, ages 2 to 3 in this portal). Everything works with pictures, sounds and a friendly voice, so no reading is needed.

👀 Sense Detective
Exploring the world with sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch. Children match each sense to the part of the body that does the job. Try each sense for real afterwards: sniff a flower, listen for a bird.
🧺 Feely Basket
Learning the words for how things feel: soft, prickly, wet, cold and hot. Fill a real basket with soft and bumpy things and talk about them together. The game reminds children never to touch fire.
🙈 Peek-a-Boo Pals
Recognising familiar animals and copying the sounds they make. The sounds are made inside the browser, so they work without internet. Say each sound together after the animal appears.
🍄 Nature Walk
Spotting and naming living things outdoors, such as insects, flowers and birds. Take a real walk afterwards and see which of them your child can find.
🏡 Home Sweet Home
Talking about family members and the everyday places and things at home. Families look different, so use your own names for the people your child knows.

How to play together: sit with your child and say the words out loud. The game cannot hear your child, so the “I said it!” button relies on you being the listener.

These activities follow the Play Group objectives for this area in the Little Scholars portal, which are based on Cambridge’s Early Years area headings. Check your school’s full framework for the exact statements. Progress is saved on this device only.
