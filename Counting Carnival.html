For grown-ups and teachers

Counting Carnival supports Mathematics in Cambridge Early Years, EY1 (ages 3 to 4). Cambridge’s Mathematics area covers developing a sense of number, handling data, and shape, space and measure, using a limited number range at each stage. This game counts from 1 to 5.

🎈 Balloon Pop
Counting out a set: one number for each balloon, then stopping at the number asked for. Sense of number.
🍎 Fruit Stall
Counting objects by touching each one, then linking the amount to its number. Sense of number.
🔷 Shape Shop
Naming common flat shapes, whatever their colour or direction, and spotting them in everyday things. Shape, space and measure.
🏁 Race Day
Using first, second and third for runners in a line 1Mn.04
🌈 Pattern Parade
Recognising and talking about patterns 1Ms.06 The “what comes next?” rounds go a step beyond EY1, towards EY2 2Ms.09
🐘 Big and Small
Comparing size, height and length with words like biggest, tallest and longest. Shape, space and measure.

How to play together: count out loud with your child and point to each thing as you say its number. Then look for patterns at home, such as stripes on a shirt or tiles on a floor, and ask “What comes next?”

The codes 1Mn.04 and 1Ms.06 (and 2Ms.09 for EY2) are quoted from Cambridge’s published Early Years curriculum samples. The other rounds follow Cambridge’s area headings. Check your school’s full framework for the complete list and for the exact number range at EY1. Progress is saved on this device only.
