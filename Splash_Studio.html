Tiny Painter
⭐0/15

For grown-ups and teachers

Splash Studio supports Expressive Arts and Design in Cambridge Early Years, for Play Group learners (Stage 1, ages 2 to 3 in this portal). Children explore colour, marks, rhythm and pretend play. There is no right or wrong picture, and no reading is needed.

🎨 Finger Paint
Experimenting with thick paint using fingers, hand prints and a sponge, on a rainbow-garden paper. Try real finger painting afterwards. The game gives more stars for using more colours, to encourage trying them.
🖍️ Squiggle Time
Making big circular and zig-zag marks with a chunky crayon. Children follow a dotted line with a finger. It is a gentle step towards pencil control, so skip any shape that frustrates your child.
🧪 Mix Magic
Discovering that mixing red, yellow and blue makes new colours. Let children try both pots in every pair. Finding out what happens matters more than getting it right first time.
👏 Clap Along
Clapping along with a steady beat, first slow, then medium, then fast. Clap with your child. Phones and tablets can have a little delay, so the game gives a generous window for each clap.
👑 Dress-Up Party
Pretend play with hats, glasses, bows and props. Ask “Who is Ellie today?” and let your child make up a story, then try a real dress-up box.

How to play together: sit with your child, name the colours and sounds out loud, and ask them to tell you about what they made.

These activities follow the Play Group objectives for this area in the Little Scholars portal, which are based on Cambridge’s Early Years area headings. Check your school’s full framework for the exact statements. Progress is saved on this device only.
