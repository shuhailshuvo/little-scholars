For grown-ups and teachers

Kindness Village supports Personal, Social and Emotional Development in Cambridge Early Years, EY1 (ages 3 to 4). Cambridge describes this area as helping children build friendships and social learning, regulate their emotional responses and respond to the needs of others, and develop self-esteem and confidence.

😊 Feelings Faces
Naming feelings and matching them to everyday moments. The game says that all feelings are okay, and invites your child to make each face.
🌬️ Calm Corner
Three slow breaths, then choosing safe ways to calm down when angry, sad or scared. Regulating emotional responses.
👍 Kind or Not?
Telling right from wrong in everyday moments 1PS.29 Cambridge notes that children at EY1 may still need adult support to respond appropriately, so talk each one through together.
💬 Magic Words
Using hello, please, thank you and sorry in real situations. Friendships and social learning.
🍪 Share Park
Sharing fairly, and seeing how it feels for each friend. Responding to the needs of others.
🧼 Daily Routines
Washing hands and getting ready for bed, step by step and in order. Looking after themselves and growing independence.
💭 After every game
Your child is asked what they liked best. This follows 1PS.06 reflecting on activities and experiences, saying what they liked and disliked.

How to play together: pause on each situation and ask, “Have you ever felt like that?” or “What would you do?” Breathe along with Ollie in Calm Corner; it works best when you do it together. Real-life practice matters more than the game.

The codes 1PS.06 and 1PS.29 are quoted from Cambridge’s published Early Years curriculum samples. The game’s rounds follow Cambridge’s description of the area and common early-years practice, not a numbered list of statements. Check your school’s full framework for the complete list.
