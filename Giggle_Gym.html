Wiggle Worm
⭐0/15

For grown-ups and teachers

Giggle Gym supports Physical Development in Cambridge Early Years, for Play Group learners (Stage 1, ages 2 to 3 in this portal). Big movement skills grow through real movement, so these games are best as a starting point for playing together away from the screen.

🚶 Tape Walk
Following a path with care, a bit like walking along a line of floor tape with a beanbag. On screen it practises steady finger control. Then stick a real tape path on the floor and let your child carry a soft beanbag along it.
🧱 Block Tower
Stacking blocks and counting them as they land. The goal is five blocks. Build a real tower afterwards, with soft blocks or cardboard boxes.
📿 Bead Threading
Picking up and threading chunky beads, and naming colours. Children can drag a bead to the string or just tap it. Real beads and string are great for small hands, but always stay close. Small beads can be a choking risk for young children.
🧼 Wash Hands
Learning the steps of hand washing before snack time: wet, soap, rub, rinse and dry. Do it at a real sink straight afterwards, and sing a short song while rubbing.
🤸 Roo Says
Active play: hop, stomp, wiggle, tiptoe, spin and stretch. Each move lasts about six seconds. Clear some space, move together, and stop whenever your child is tired.

How to play together: do the moves yourself, cheer each other on, and let your child set the pace.

These activities follow the Play Group objectives for this area in the Little Scholars portal, which are based on Cambridge’s Early Years area headings. Check your school’s full framework for the exact statements. Progress is saved on this device only.
