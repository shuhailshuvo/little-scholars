For grown-ups and teachers

Chatterbox Island supports Communication, Language and Literacy in Cambridge Early Years, EY1 (ages 3 to 4). Every island is about listening and talking first, so no reading is needed.

🎶 Rhyme Time
Joining in with familiar nursery rhymes 1CLc.06 1CLc.14. Children finish each rhyme, then hear and see the whole line.
🎧 Sound Safari
Listening carefully and telling sounds apart, an early step toward phonics. Children match animal and instrument sounds to pictures.
🗣️ Picture Talk
Listening and responding to questions 1CLc.02 1CLc.08, naming things and saying them out loud. Words come from the EY1 themes All about me, Senses, and Food and things we grow.
🌻 Story Garden
Enjoying and beginning to listen attentively to a story 1CLc.05. Doing an action at key words keeps little listeners involved, which fits Cambridge’s EY1 guidance.
🖍️ Magic Crayon
Drawing and describing a best friend 1CLc.18 1CLc.37. Mark-making comes first, then talking about the picture in their own words.

How to play together: sit with your child and say the words out loud. The game cannot hear your child, so the “I said it!” and “Tell me” buttons rely on you being the listener. Ask follow-up questions such as “What colour is it?” or “What is happening?”

Statement codes come from Cambridge’s published EY1 long-term plan. Check your school’s full framework for the complete list. Progress is saved on this device only.
